## New finding

### 1. Low: the analysis does not check other A2 attempts' receipts (src/seg_a2_analysis.py:277-312 and 253-274)

D-0072 makes several attempts per seed a normal event. The analysis takes the one evaluation root supplied for each seed and checks that root's single receipt. It never looks at the other declared attempts. So nothing in the locked analysis shows that the chosen root is the only complete opened attempt for its seed under this lock.

The runbook asks for an audit of all receipts only in the same-profile case. An accidental duplicate submission, or a rerun after an engineering failure that had already opened A2, could leave two complete results for one seed with the choice between them unrecorded. The runbook's procedure makes this unlikely, and it does not affect rules 5 and 6.

**Fix.** In `analyze`, for every attempt in `accounted_attempts`:
- Read research/artifacts/<attempt>/manifest.json, plus `a2-read.jsonl` if it exists.
- Collect every attempt whose receipt or manifest names this lock's SHA256.
- Require that each seed has exactly one complete opened attempt, and that it is the supplied root.
- Require that every other opened attempt is partial.
- Output the list of opened attempts per seed.

The `dev_replay` and `retry_profile_miss` manifests could also be required to appear in the accounting list, so that the accounting is checked rather than only described.

**Verify.** A synthetic test with two complete opened attempts for one seed, which must be rejected. A second test where a partial opened attempt plus one complete attempt is accepted and listed.
