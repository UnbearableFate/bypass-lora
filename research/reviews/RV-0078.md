## Finding 1 (low, nonblocking): two analysis direction labels misstate the locked next step

Location: src/seg_rules.py:79-83 and :56-60.

**(i) A pending r128 is overwritten.**
- Suppose the primary task's gain is in (0, 3), so r128 is pending and `release_r128` is True, while the secondary task reaches "supports" at r32.
- Then lines 81-83 overwrite `pending_r128` with `inconclusive_secondary_only`, and rule 8 reports `two_new_seed_replications_needed: True`.
- Example: `grid(gain=10)` with `second_gain=30` and a BoolQ swap drop of 15 gives direction `inconclusive_secondary_only` together with `release_r128` True.
- Rule 8 applies when "主任务不成立", but the primary outcome is not decided until r128 runs. The report then names two conflicting next actions.
- test_secondary_only_cannot_create_primary_edge_or_release covers only a primary gain of 0.

**(ii) The D-0044(c) case is labelled "descriptive".** After r128, D-0044(c) says "其余情况只作描述 … H-0019 记 inconclusive". The code reports this case as plain `descriptive`, the same label as the r32 descriptive cases, which carry no inconclusive mandate.

Fix:
- Compute `secondary_only` only when the primary task is decided, i.e. not when `direction == 'pending_r128'`. Report the secondary result under rule 8 and set `two_new_seed_replications_needed` only after the primary is decided.
- Emit a distinct label such as `descriptive_inconclusive_D0044c` when r128 was completed and neither (a) nor (b) holds.

Verify: add rule tests for (1) primary gain 1 pp with secondary support, expecting `pending_r128` with release True and no replication flag, and (2) D-0044(c), expecting the new label.

## Finding 2 (low, nonblocking): the `select` stage reports missing interventions as a failed implementation

Location: src/seg_analysis.py:64-70 and :100-103. CLI choices are at :138.

- At `--stage select` a KEEP arm without interventions sets `off_ok = False`. Then `off_matches_base` is False and `implementation_ok` is False.
- The direction becomes `invalid_implementation`, rule 1's action becomes `repair_no_conclusion`, and `release_r128` is False.
- So a select-stage report always says the implementation failed and cannot show the rule-6 release, even when the implementation is fine.
- The intended workflow does not need this stage: run the final stage on the r32 grid with SEG-02 interventions, which yields `pending_r128` and release True. But the stage is exposed in the CLI and in run.pbs (`SEG_ANALYSIS_PHASE`), and a reader could stop on its output.

Fix: either remove `select`, or represent a missing intervention as "not yet assessed" (for example `off_matches_base: None` with direction `awaiting_interventions`) rather than False.

Verify: a select-stage synthetic analysis does not report `invalid_implementation` when every recorded check passed.
