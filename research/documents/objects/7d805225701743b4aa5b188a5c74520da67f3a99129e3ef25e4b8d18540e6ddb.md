# RV-0075 answer (reviewer-3): conclusion review, H-0014@1 -> challenged (screening)

The records support recording H-0014@1 as challenged at the screening level, for seed 0 and the RB1 setting only.

- **The rule was fixed before the data.** Rule 4 (GH) and rule 11 come from the sealed E-0021@1 contract, sealed 2026-11-01T19:14. D-0032 followed at 19:33, and the first training attempt was registered at 20:00. The current E-0021.md digest matches DOC-0235@1 (blake2b c2b60e2a…).
- **The direction follows from the rule.** The primary history contrast RB1-02 − RB1-01 is −0.20 pp, which is ≤ 0, so rule 11 records "challenges".
- **The interval also falls short of the threshold.** Its upper bound, +0.90 pp, is below the +1 pp the hypothesis requires. This makes the case stronger than the D-0004 precedent: H-0002 was recorded as challenged (screening) with a +0.35 pp point estimate and an upper bound of 1.35 pp.
- **No engineering failure or tuning asymmetry explains the null T0 contrast.**
  - Both arms finished, are effective (≥ Base + 1), reload identically, and show 0 prediction mismatches between normal and training evaluation.
  - RB1-02's own branch carries its gain: iv_off = Base gives −6.95 pp.
  - Both arms share the plan order, the token and step counts, and the 663,552 parameters.
  - The LR lock favours the memory arm, if anything. RBc-R's 3e-3 is an interior minimum, while RBc-T's 3e-3 sits at the upper grid boundary and was not expanded further (T-0024 delivery). So T0 is the arm that might be under-tuned.

All the numbers in the conclusion match the records:
- **RES-0228 and RES-0229.** RB1-01 is 73.3/81.3 (macro 77.30) and RB1-02 is 72.4/81.8 (macro 77.10). The difference is −0.20 pp macro: PIQA −0.90, BoolQ +0.50.
- **analysis.json bootstrap** (paired, task-stratified, 10,000 repeats, seed 20261101, percentile):
  - RB1-01 − RB1-02 = +0.002 [−0.009, +0.013], which gives the stated [−1.30, +0.90].
  - Swap drop: 1.85 [0.75, 3.00].
  - Permute drop: 17.15 [14.70, 19.60].

The three findings below are wording and presentation points. None changes the status or the scope.

## Finding 1 (low, overclaim): "达到同样的准确率" claims equivalence

The proposed conclusion says "但不带这段历史训练的模型达到同样的准确率". The records do not support an equivalence claim:
- T0 − RB1-02 is +0.20 pp, and its descriptive interval is [−0.90, +1.30].
- That interval resamples dev rows only. It excludes seed and selection variability, and only one seed was run.
- EV-0119 itself says the result "does not establish beneficial memory or equivalence".

What the records do support is this: T0's point estimate is not below RB1-02's, and even the descriptive interval excludes the ≥ +1 pp RB1-02 advantage the hypothesis requires.

Suggested wording: "但不带侧支历史训练的同参数 T0 在点估计上不低于 RB1-02（RB1-02 − RB1-01 = −0.20 pp；描述性区间 [−1.30, +0.90] 的上限也低于 H-0014 要求的 +1 pp；区间只含 dev 行重采样，不含 seed 变异，不表示两者等效；T0 仍经原 attention 与含上下文的 Q/K/V 获得上下文）".

## Finding 2 (low, overclaim / unruled alternative): the permute drop is cited as evidence of using side history

The proposed conclusion says "冻结历史干预确实降低 RB1-02（swap −1.85 pp …，permute −17.15 pp …），说明训练后的 RB1-02 在使用侧支历史". The interventions do show that the trained model's outputs depend on its side state. But H-0014's own scope warns that interventions may shift the distribution and that a single zero/shuffle drop is not mechanism evidence. The diagnostics in DOC-0263 point to that alternative for the large drops:

- **RB1-02's reads at answer-predicting positions are concentrated.**
  - Excluding BoolQ layer 6, nonempty n_eff is 1.11–4.83 and relative n_eff is 0.010–0.080.
  - Current-token weight is ≤ 0.011 at every layer.
  - iv_strict changes nothing: +0.05 pp [−0.60, +0.70].
  - Truncating the history is catastrophic: window1 gives −30.05 pp and window8 gives −14.00 pp.
- **The large drops may come from a broken state structure.** Permuting key/value pairing, or truncating the history, removes the few prefix positions the trained branch relies on. The 17.15 pp permute drop may therefore mostly reflect that structure being broken (distribution shift) rather than episode-specific content. I did not inspect per-position weights, so this is an inference from the aggregate diagnostics.
- **Swap is the more direct measure of content dependence.** It keeps a natural history and changes only its episode, and it gives −1.85 pp [−3.00, −0.75].

Note also that the intervention conjunct of H-0014 met its pre-registered thresholds (1.85 and 17.15, both ≥ 1). The challenge therefore rests entirely on the T0 conjunct, which plan2 §11.3 makes the decisive control for exactly this reason. Saying so explicitly keeps a reader from concluding that both parts failed.

Suggested wording: "训练后的 RB1-02 的输出依赖侧支状态：换成同任务其他 episode 的历史使宏平均降低 1.85 pp [0.75, 3.00]（H-0014 的干预部分达到各 ≥ 1 pp）；打乱 key/value 配对降低 17.15 pp，但读权重集中在少数非当前位置，这一幅度可能主要来自状态结构被破坏（分布偏移），不作侧支内容带来收益的证据。挑战只来自 T0 对照。"

## Finding 3 (low, presentation): RES-0243 and RES-0247 show `false` for controls that were not applicable

- RES-0247 (RB1-01) records `"iv_off_identical_to_Base": false`.
- RES-0243 (RB1-02) records `"T0_swap_noop": false`.

Read from the package, both look like failed controls. They are not.
- In src/rb1_intervene.py:156-157 the flags are set purely by arm: `args.arm != 'RB1-01'` and `args.arm == 'RB1-01'`.
- RB1-01 runs only iv_swap (line 125; its conditions list is normal, Base_same_allocation, iv_swap).
- The real checks raise on failure: iv_off ≠ Base at line 132, and the T0 swap not being a no-op at line 134.

EV-0119's statement that "Frozen off/Base and T0 swap identities pass" is correct: RB1-02 iv_off equals Base (−6.95 pp), and RB1-01 iv_swap is 0.000.

Suggested fix: in the decision rationale, state which control ran on which arm and that `false` here means "not run for this arm". Optionally add a note to RES-0243 and RES-0247.
