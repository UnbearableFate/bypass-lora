# RV-0052 answer (reviewer-3): conclusion review, H-0011 challenged

Verdict: **issues**. 2 findings, 1 of them medium.

The numbers in the proposed conclusion match the run. The screening level fits, and "challenged" is supported for H-0011 as registered (D3 trained from scratch). The record needs to say what kind of failure this was. As worded, it suggests the marginal objective was exercised on addressing and failed to prevent collapse. The records show something else: the per-slot decoder never became fact-specific. So the addressing half of H-0011 got essentially no training signal, and the warm-start alternative remains open.

## Finding 1 (medium, unruled_alternative / out_of_scope): D3 fell into the answer-prior solution with a dead decoder, so the addressing claim was not exercised

- Location: the proposed H-0011 conclusion (the wording "塌缩没有避免", "陈述的两个部分…都不成立", and the reopen conditions), based on EV-0100 / ATT-0155-1.
- Evidence (ATT-0155-1 `arm/metrics.json` and ATT-0169-1 `report.md`):
  - **The decoder never became fact-specific.** Reading the target slot's value does not help. Hard `oracle_value` is 17/1000 with NLL 4.421358, against hard `normal` at 15/1000 and 4.424336. Soft `oracle_value` gives the same 17/1000 and 4.421.
  - **Memory content barely matters.** Every control that reads a nonzero memory ends at NLL 4.421–4.429, including shuffled (4.428) and permuted (4.428). Only zero memory differs (9.248).
  - **This is the known answer-prior solution.** It is the level the E-0016 design calls the collapse solution (4.42; AD-0002 §1.3). It also matches E-0014 R4 (EV-0083): NLL 4.4303 = shuffled, target weight 0.061.
  - **It is not undertraining.** Training marginal CE was 4.52 by steps 2001–2500 and 4.41 at the end. On the 6000-step cosine with a 300-step warmup, the LR at step 2500 is still about 2e-3, so the run was stuck while the LR was still high.
  - **Why addressing got no signal.** When p(answer | v_i) is the same for every slot, the gradient of −log Σ w_i p_i with respect to w is about zero.
  - **The weights stayed close to uniform.** Last-position target weight is 0.061 ≈ 1/16, and last-position n_eff is 7.89 of 16. The 775/1000 argmax on slot 0 is a tilt within near-uniform weights. It is not the sharp single-slot collapse of R4 (100 % on slot 0).
  - **The warm-start alternative is open.** D1 shows a decoder is learnable in this setup (tf_normal 668/1000). D2a and D2b tested warm-start addressing only with the mixture CE (+3.2 and +3.0 pp). Marginal likelihood starting from an informative decoder was never run.
- Impact: as worded, the record would read as "the marginal objective does not stop slot collapse or let addressing be discovered". What D3 supports is narrower: from a cold start, marginal training at this budget converges to the answer-prior solution before any per-slot decoder forms.
- Fix (wording only; no re-run needed for this record):
  1. Add the failure mode: "oracle_value 17/1000 (NLL 4.421) ≈ normal; all nonzero-memory controls at the answer-prior level 4.42–4.43; the per-slot decoder did not learn, so the marginal objective gave the address almost no signal (last-position target weight 0.061 ≈ 1/16, n_eff 7.9)".
  2. Replace "塌缩没有避免" with "最后位置 argmax 77.5% 落在槽位 0（权重近均匀，n_eff 7.9），未出现可用寻址".
  3. Scope the challenge to "从零训练（无解码器预热）".
  4. Add a reopen condition: "若需要在已有可用解码器（如 D1 checkpoint）上检验边际似然能否发现寻址".
